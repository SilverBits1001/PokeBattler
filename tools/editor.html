How it works

Solid shapes are invisible cover: fighters and shots stop at them (pillars, statues, rocks). The game fills them with the round colliders shown as circles.

Water shapes are standing pools: everyone in them is slowed, and Electric damage in them shocks the water.

Layer shapes redraw that part of the painting over fighters. Sorted layers hide fighters standing behind their base line (the gold line: drag it), so they can walk behind a pillar. Always in front layers cover everyone.

Draw with Rectangle, Ellipse (drag) or Polygon (click points; double-click, Enter or click the first point to finish; Esc cancels). Select to move, drag the white handles to resize, arrows to nudge (Shift for 8px), Delete to remove, Ctrl+D to duplicate. In a polygon, double-click an edge to add a point; select a point and press Delete to remove it.

The test fighter (a 9px body like the game's) shows how solid shapes push it and whether a layer covers it.
