# Roster 3: seven fighters, three legendaries, and a rebalance

Each new fighter has one idea at the centre of its kit. That idea comes from the
Pokémon's lore and typing, and it sets how the fighter wins, how it loses, and
what it costs to play it well. "Balanced" here means risk matches reward. It
does not mean equal damage: the big payoffs need a commitment that a sharp
opponent can punish.

Controls recap: **A** basic 3-hit combo, **RS** smash (hold to charge),
**X** / **Y** specials on cooldowns, **B** dodge, plus one passive ability.

---

## Fixes to the current roster

### Metagross (Steel / Psychic): moves that match its typing

Its kit used Hammer Arm (Fighting) and Magnet Rise (Electric). The new kit is
all Steel and Psychic. It keeps the slow, armoured bruiser identity: a
supercomputer with four brains, built for crushing punches and mind powers.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Bullet fists | STEEL | Fast iron jabs, then a heavy slam. |
| RS | **Meteor Mash** | STEEL | An armoured comet punch. If it lands, Metagross gets a short attack boost ("the meteor rides on"). |
| X | Zen Headbutt | PSYCHIC | Kept: a lunging headbutt that can flinch. |
| Y | **Psychic** | PSYCHIC | A telekinetic pull. The nearest foe in a cone is yanked into fist range and briefly held. Whiffing costs a long recovery. |
| * | Clear Body | — | Kept: never slowed. |

Risk vs reward: Psychic sets up Meteor Mash. Both have big windups, so a read
wins the round and a whiff gets punished.

### Snorlax: rebalance

It was armoured on every jab, so mashing A beat almost every interrupt. That
felt oppressive up close and gave its slow tank style no real weakness.

- Only the third combo hit (Stomp) keeps armour. The two jabs come out one
  frame faster to compensate.
- The combo, Body Slam and Heavy Slam are tagged NORMAL, so its damage reads as
  its own type.
- The HP and damage scale are retuned against the sims.

### Froslass: nerf

Two of her three combo hits chilled. Together with a short-cooldown Icy Wind,
she could chain freezes. Her damage scale (1.53) was also the highest in the
game.

- Only the combo finisher chills.
- Icy Wind cooldown 90 → 120.
- Freeze 42 → 34 ticks.
- Frost immunity after a freeze 240 → 300 ticks.
- Damage scale 1.53 → 1.38.
- Cursed Body chance 30% → 20%.

## The seven new fighters

### Lurantis (Grass): territory duelist

*Lore:* the Bloom Sickle Pokémon, a flamboyant mantis that draws power from
sunlight. Signature move: Solar Blade.

*Play style:* plant your sun and duel inside it. Lurantis has the longest melee
reach in the game, but its strongest move is slow outside its sunlight.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Leaf Blade | GRASS | Three long scythe sweeps. Leaf Blade can land a critical hit (×1.5, 15%). |
| RS | **Solar Blade** | GRASS | A huge sweeping blade with the longest reach of any smash. Outside the sun it needs a long charge before it can fire. Inside its sun patch, it fires at full power instantly. |
| X | X-Scissor | BUG | A crossing dash slash that passes through the foe. |
| Y | **Sunny Day** | FIRE | Plants a sunlit circle for 8 s, one at a time. While Lurantis stands in it, Synthesis heals it slowly. |
| B | Petal step | — | A short dash. |
| * | Leaf Guard | — | Immune to burn, chill, paralysis and daze while in its sun. |

Risk vs reward: away from the sun it is a slow, readable swordsman. On its own
turf it is the scariest duelist in the game. Opponents should pull it out of
the light.

### Chandelure (Ghost / Fire): burn zoner

*Lore:* a chandelier that burns the spirits it absorbs, and hypnotises with its
flames.

*Play style:* keep away, burn everything, then cash the burns in with Hex. It
floats and is fragile, and its big blast leaves it drained.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Flame flicks | FIRE | Short-range ember puffs; weak but safe. |
| RS | **Overheat** | FIRE | A massive fire burst in a cone. Afterwards Chandelure's damage drops by 30% for 4 s. |
| X | **Will-O-Wisp** | FIRE | Three slow, homing ghost flames that burn on contact. |
| Y | **Hex** | GHOST | A curse flame bursts on the target spot after a short delay. Double damage to a burned foe. |
| B | Phase | — | Turns intangible and drifts (a blink). |
| * | Flash Fire | — | Fire hits deal half damage and power up its next Fire move by 30%. |

Floats: it does not touch the ground, so it ignores ground hazards and ground
quakes.

Risk vs reward: lowest close-range threat and low HP, with high payoff from
setup. Overheat is a finisher with a real cost.

### Ceruledge (Fire / Ghost): lifesteal berserker

*Lore:* a fallen warrior's armour, with blades of ghostly fire. Its signature
Bitter Blade drains life.

*Play style:* stay in their face. It heals by hitting, gets faster when it is
beaten on, and can throw its blades away for reach, though that leaves it
unarmed for a moment.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Spectral blades | GHOST | Two quick cuts, then a fire cross-slash. |
| RS | **Bitter Blade** | FIRE | A lunging double slash. It heals Ceruledge for 50% of the damage dealt. |
| X | Flame Charge | FIRE | A dashing tackle. Each hit grants +6% speed, up to 3 stacks, lasting 6 s. |
| Y | **Poltergeist** | GHOST | Hurls both blades as a returning boomerang that hits on the way out and back. Until they return, its A combo deals 35% less. |
| B | Blade step | — | A short dash. |
| * | Weak Armor | — | A heavy hit on it gives +15% speed but +8% damage taken for 3 s. |

Risk vs reward: it needs hits to sustain itself. Ranged foes who avoid it starve
it.

### Volcarona (Bug / Fire): set-up snowballer

*Lore:* the Sun Pokémon. Its fire scales once replaced the sun for people
during a winter of volcanic ash. Its signature set-up is Quiver Dance.

*Play style:* dance to power up, then take over. Every Quiver Dance stack makes
it stronger and faster, but the dance is long and can be interrupted, and a
hard hit knocks a stack off.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Scale gusts | BUG | Wing gusts with short range. |
| RS | **Fiery Dance** | FIRE | A ring of flame that bursts outward from it; its radius grows with charge. A hit has a 50% chance to grant a Quiver stack. |
| X | Bug Buzz | BUG | A sound-wave cone at mid range. |
| Y | **Quiver Dance** | BUG | A 0.7 s dance (interruptible) that grants one stack, up to 3. Each stack gives +12% damage and +8% speed. At 3 stacks, burning scales fall around it. |
| B | Flutter | — | An air dodge. |
| * | Flame Body | — | Contact with it can burn the attacker (30%). |

Floats.

Risk vs reward: at 0 stacks it is a below-average fighter. At 3 it is a raid
boss. Losing a stack to a heavy hit, and the vulnerable dance, are the price.

### Milotic (Water): sustain controller

*Lore:* the most beautiful Pokémon, said to calm hostility. Its ability is
Marvel Scale.

*Play style:* a long serpent body that controls space with whirlpools and
out-lasts you with Aqua Ring.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Aqua Tail | WATER | Long-reach tail whips; the third hit is a wide sweep. |
| RS | **Whirlpool** | WATER | A vortex at range (the distance grows with charge). It pulls foes in and damages them over 2 s. |
| X | Scald | WATER | A boiling water shot that burns (30%). |
| Y | **Aqua Ring** | WATER | A ring of water that heals 1.6% max HP per second for 6 s and washes off chill and burn. |
| B | Coil slide | — | A sliding dash. |
| * | Marvel Scale | — | Takes 25% less damage while statused (burned, chilled, poisoned, paralysed or dazed). |

Risk vs reward: low burst. It wins long fights through control and healing.
Rushdown that refuses to get trapped beats it.

### Inteleon (Water): sniper

*Lore:* a secret agent that fires water from its fingertips at Mach 3 and
glides on a hidden membrane. Its signature is Snipe Shot, and its ability is
Sniper.

*Play style:* make space, then line up the shot. A laser sight telegraphs
every Snipe Shot.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Agent jabs | WATER | Quick and weak; it gets you out. |
| RS | **Snipe Shot** | WATER | Hold to aim; a laser sight tracks the target. On release an instant water bullet crosses the whole arena. A full charge is a critical (Sniper: ×1.5). |
| X | Water Gun | WATER | A three-round fingertip burst. |
| Y | **Acrobatics** | FLYING | A long backward glide with a kick on take-off, untouchable in the air. Its next Snipe Shot charges twice as fast. |
| B | Agent roll | — | A quick dash. |
| * | Sniper | — | Critical Snipe Shots deal 1.5× more. |

Risk vs reward: the highest single hit in the game, with a long, visible charge
and the weakest close-range game.

### Zoroark (Dark): illusionist

*Lore:* the Illusion Fox, which takes the form of other Pokémon. Its signature
move is Night Daze.

*Play style:* mind games. It starts every round disguised as another Pokémon.
Its first strike from a disguise is an ambush that hits harder. It can vanish
into a decoy and re-disguise.

| Slot | Move | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Fury claws | DARK | A fast claw string. |
| RS | **Foul Play** | DARK | A lunging strike that uses the target's own power: the stronger the foe's kit, the harder it hits. |
| X | **Night Daze** | DARK | A dark shockwave around it that dazes. |
| Y | **Illusion** | DARK | Leaves a decoy Zoroark that runs forward and bursts when hit. Zoroark turns briefly invisible, then reappears disguised for 5 s. |
| B | Feint step | — | A dash. |
| * | Illusion | — | Starts each round disguised. An attack from a disguise ends it and hits for +30% (ambush). Taking a hit also breaks the disguise. |

Risk vs reward: medium-low HP with a lot of guessing. It rewards reads and
punishes predictable opponents.

## The three new legendaries

Each one follows the existing Final Smash flow (activation freeze, opening hit,
then a cinematic for anyone caught). Each one also takes over the whole
background with its own world.

### Dialga (Steel / Dragon): Roar of Time

*World:* time itself. The arena drains to sepia blue. A giant clock face and
turning gears fill the sky, and the clock hands stop.

*Effect:* time stops. Every foe within a wide radius of the aim point is frozen
in place (shown in greyscale) for 1.5 s. Then the Roar of Time beam erupts
through all of them. Time also rewinds for the caller: it gets back 40% of the
HP it lost in the last 5 s.

*Cinematic:* a clock-face backdrop, ticking hits as the gears grind, and the
roar as the finisher.

### Palkia (Water / Dragon): Spacial Rend

*World:* deep space. Stars, a pearl-pink nebula and a fractured horizon.

*Effect:* space tears. Three spatial rifts slash across the arena through the
aim point in quick succession. Foes cut by a rift are pulled to its centre
line and take a heavy hit.

*Cinematic:* the battle cuts to open space and the caught foes float. Palkia
cuts the void three times, then space snaps shut on them as the finisher.

### Giratina (Ghost / Dragon, Origin Forme): Shadow Force

*World:* the Distortion World. It is purple-black, with upside-down floating
islands, twisting stone and rippling space.

*Effect:* Giratina sinks into the Distortion World. A shadow hunts the nearest
foe across the floor, then it erupts beneath them. It goes through guard,
counters, shields and invulnerability. Everyone near the eruption is dragged
into the Distortion World for the cinematic.

*Cinematic:* an inverted world with tendrils, ghostly hits, then the eruption
as the finisher.

## What testing changed

These results come from CPU round robins with every pairing, 2 games per pairing on
each of three arenas (Stadium, Ruins, Frostfall), plus Free-for-All and 2v2 samples.

- **Duels:** every one of the 25 fighters averages 40–61%.
  - The new ones: Chandelure 0.53, Milotic 0.51, Volcarona 0.49, Inteleon 0.48,
    Lurantis 0.45, Ceruledge 0.44, Zoroark 0.41.
  - Volcarona and Zoroark sit low against the CPU. Their payoffs (set-up, mind
    games) reward a person more than the CPU, so they were not pushed higher.
- **Froslass** ended at damage scale 1.5, not 1.38: the chill and freeze changes
  alone took her from about 0.50 to 0.40. She now sits just under average.
- **Snorlax** without jab armour dropped to 0.36, so its damage scale went
  0.82 → 0.92. It is back to about 0.48.
- **Metagross** with Psychic and Meteor Mash rose to about 0.57. Its damage scale went
  0.9 → 0.82, and Psychic's stun is 20 ticks.
- **Weak Armor** lost its damage-taken penalty: heavy hits are so common that the
  penalty made Ceruledge a free kill. It is now a speed boost only.
- **Volcarona**'s second combo hit is Fire, not Bug. Bug is resisted widely and
  Volcarona takes 4× from Rock. A Quiver stack is lost only to a hit of 40+ damage.
  The dance is 0.5 s and each stack gives +18% damage.
- **Milotic**'s Whirlpool pull and damage were trimmed (it won 11 of 12 at first).
- **Free-for-All and 2v2** have per-Pokémon tables for the new fighters.
  - Zoners are scaled down in Free-for-All.
  - Chandelure and Milotic take more damage there, since floating and Aqua Ring
    outlast a crowd.
  - Ceruledge, Inteleon and Zoroark get a little more in 2v2.
- **Online:** a rollback replay of every new kit and legendary on all five arenas
  reproduces the simulation exactly (snapshot, play, restore, replay, compare). The
  online version is 9.
- **Engine fixes found on the way:**
  - Beams (Ice Beam, Overheat and the rest) no longer vanish while their user is
    in hitstop.
  - Fire as a second type now blocks burns.
